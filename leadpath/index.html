LeadPath Digital is a digital marketing agency that manages Google Ads, search engine optimisation and Google Business Profiles for local service businesses — turning searches into calls, leads and customers.

What we do

Google Ads management

Search campaign setup, keyword and negative keyword management, ad copy, budgets and conversion tracking.

Google Business Profile

Profile optimisation, local Maps visibility, review monitoring and responses.

SEO

Website SEO audits, keyword research, on-page improvements and organic search reporting.

Reporting

Clear monthly reports on spend, leads, cost per lead, rankings and reviews.

How we work

  1. Connect. With the client's permission, we link their Google Ads account to our manager account and connect their Search Console and Business Profile.

  2. Analyse. Our internal reporting tool reviews campaign, search and profile performance to find wasted spend and opportunities.

  3. Recommend. Every proposed change — budgets, keywords, ads — is explained with the data behind it.

  4. Approve. No change is made to a live campaign until it has been reviewed and approved.
