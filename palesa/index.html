    FAQ

    Questions Customers Ask

    Can't find what you're looking for? Ask us directly in your quote request.

    How do I request a quotation?

    Fill in the quotation form on this page. When you press the button, WhatsApp opens with your details written out as a message — press Send and we'll reply to discuss the job. You can also chat to us on WhatsApp directly.

    What information do you need for an installation?

    Your suburb or town, the type of property, which service you need, whether you already have equipment, and how many TVs should be connected. If you're not sure about the technical details, that's fine — describe what you want in your own words. Photos of your current setup can help, and you can send them in the WhatsApp chat after your message.

    Can you help with an existing DStv signal problem?

    Yes. Signal problems are often caused by a dish that has moved out of alignment, a faulty LNB, or damaged or loose cabling, and these can be checked on site. Problems related to your subscription or account need to be resolved with DStv directly.

    Can I request additional TV points?

    Yes. Additional TV points and Extra View setups depend on your existing dish, decoder and how your home is laid out. Tell us how many rooms you'd like connected and what equipment you currently have, and we'll advise on what is possible.

    Do you supply the equipment as well as the installation?

    It depends on the job. Let us know in your request whether you already have a decoder, dish or cabling, and we'll confirm what equipment would be needed and include it in the quotation where applicable.

    How soon can an installation be arranged?

    That depends on your location, the type of work and availability at the time. Add a preferred date and how urgent the job is in your request, and we'll discuss timing when we contact you. A submitted request is not a confirmed booking — the appointment is arranged once the quotation is agreed.

    Which areas do you service?

    Tell us your suburb or town in the quotation form and we'll confirm whether we can assist in your area.

    How will I know the final cost?

    You'll receive a quotation after we understand the job. The cost depends on the property, the equipment needed, cable runs, roof access and how complex the installation or repair is. We'll discuss the quotation with you before any work begins.
