About Palesa Visuals

Installations and repairs for homes and businesses across Johannesburg and surrounding areas.

Palesa Visuals installs, aligns and repairs DStv, and sets up the TVs, cameras, projectors and sound that go with it. Every job is assessed first, quoted clearly and tested before we leave.

Why Palesa Visuals

What you can expect from us.

A setup that is rushed tends to come back as a signal problem. We take the time to assess the job, explain what is needed and finish the work neatly, so your setup keeps working long after we've gone.

  1. 01

    A proper assessment first

    We look at your property, the dish position and your existing equipment before recommending anything.

  2. 02

    Clear communication before work begins

    You know what will be done and why before anyone climbs a ladder.

  3. 03

    Careful cable routing and finishing

    Cables are routed and secured neatly, not left hanging across walls and windows.

  4. 04

    The right equipment for the job

    Brackets, cabling and connectors suited to your installation and the number of TVs.

  5. 05

    Tested before we finish

    Signal and picture are checked on every connected TV point before the job is complete.

  6. 06

    A quotation based on your needs

    Your quote reflects the work your setup actually requires, discussed with you upfront.
